▶ VOXELBEAT SELF-TEST

Standalone diagnostic harness — doesn't modify index.html. Loads your real site in a hidden iframe and exercises it programmatically. Must be served over http(s):// from the same origin as index.html (e.g. your GitHub Pages URL) — opening this file directly as file:// will fail for the same cross-origin reason YouTube playback needs real hosting.

Blind Click Test

Clicks every button, flips every checkbox, drags every slider to max, and focuses/types every text field — cold, with nothing loaded. Catches wiring bugs (broken getElementById, undefined functions, thrown errors). Won't catch silent wrong-behavior, since most controls early-exit when there's no track/data loaded.
